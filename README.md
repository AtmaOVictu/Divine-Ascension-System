## Tension and Transitions

Agents hold a level of **tension** that, along with emotion, drives their
transition between ontological states. When the tension exceeds `0.7` or the
emotion is above `0.5`, the agent advances from **Ego** to **Jaz** or from **Jaz**
to **Enotnost**.

After every successful transition the agent's tension is multiplied by a decay
factor (`0.5` by default). This decay prevents the agent from transitioning
multiple times in quick succession unless tension builds up again through
interaction.
